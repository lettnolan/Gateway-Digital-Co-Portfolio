Who We Are

A St. Louis Web Design Studio Built for Local Business

Gateway Digital Co. is a web design studio based in St. Louis, Missouri, focused on one thing: helping local businesses get online with sites that actually work.

We started because too many great St. Louis businesses were either stuck with outdated websites or paying agency prices they couldn't justify. We offer agency-quality design at a price that makes sense for a local business owner — without the runaround.

Every site we build is hand-coded from scratch — no page builders, no bloated themes, no templates. That means your site loads fast, looks sharp, and doesn't break every time you sneeze near it.
